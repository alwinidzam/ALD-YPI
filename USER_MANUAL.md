# User Manual

Welcome to the ALD (Arsip Laporan Digital) User Manual. This guide will help you navigate and utilize the system effectively.

## 1. Installing the App (PWA)
For the best experience, install ALD directly to your device:
- **Android (Chrome):** Open the app in your browser, tap the menu (three dots), and select **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install App"**.
- **iOS (Safari):** Open the app, tap the Share icon, and select **"Add to Home Screen"**.

## 2. Scanning Documents
To quickly digitize physical reports:
1. Tap the **+** (Plus) button in the navigation bar.
2. Select **Scan Kamera**.
3. Align the document within the frame and capture.
4. The system will automatically convert it to a PDF and queue it for upload.

## 3. Viewing Documents
1. Navigate to your institution's folder or use the global search bar.
2. Tap on any document to open it in the built-in PDF Viewer.
3. You can zoom, pan, and read documents seamlessly. For detailed metadata, tap the info icon.

## 4. Scanning Attendance (Staff)
For daily attendance logging:
1. Navigate to the **Scanner** tab.
2. Grant camera permissions if prompted.
3. Hold your Staff QR Code in front of the camera.
4. The system will emit a beep and vibrate upon successful check-in.

## 5. Working Offline
ALD is designed to work even when you lose internet connection.
- You can continue to view previously opened documents.
- You can still scan attendance or queue document uploads.
- The system will show a sync indicator at the bottom of the screen. Once you regain an internet connection, all offline actions will automatically synchronize with the server.
